# UI/UX Design Decisions Log (`docs/UI_DECISIONS.md`)

*Started: 2026-11-21 · Tracks every conservative decision made where `Mario-Style UI UX Design Spec.md` was silent or marked `[V]`.*

| ID | Spec Ref | Original Tag | Conservative Decision | Design Token / Setting | Rationale | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DEC-01** | §2.3 (Coin 1-UP rollover) | `[V]` | Regular currency (**Gold Shards**) rolls over at `100` to grant `+1 Life` (capped at `99` shared lives). | `TOKENS.rules.coinsPerLife = 100`, `TOKENS.rules.maxLives = 99` | Matches existing `MovementProfile.collectibles.shardsPerOneUp = 100` and preserves classic platformer economy clarity without breaking counter semantics. | 2026-11-21 |
| **DEC-02** | §2.3 (Countdown timer) | `[V]` | Make `Timer` optional and toggleable (`timerEnabled`, default `true` in classic campaign courses, `false` in free/exploration modes). Warning triggers at `< 100s` (red + pulse + `!` glyph), final `10s` triggers `timerWarning` beep hook, `0s` enters `expired` state. | `TOKENS.rules.timerWarningSec = 100`, `TOKENS.rules.timerFinalBeepSec = 10` | Satisfies both timed platformer courses (NSMBU/3DW) and timerless Wonder-style play via a single non-destructive setting. | 2026-11-21 |
| **DEC-03** | §2.4 (Corner placement) | `[V]` | Fixed conservative corner mapping inside the 4% safe margin band: **Top-Left** = Survival (`LivesBadge`, `LifeMeter`, `ReserveItemBox`); **Top-Centre** = Objective (`CollectibleTracker`, 3 Star Medallion slots); **Top-Right** = Wealth (`Gold Shards`, `Prism Petals`) + Context (`Course Code`, optional `Timer`). **Bottom-Left / Bottom-Right** = transient contextual `Prompt` / `Toast` only. | `TOKENS.layout.safeMargin = '4vh'`, `TOKENS.layout.slots.*` | Strictly enforces §3.1 grouping rule (never interleaves Survival, Wealth, Objective, Context) and keeps the centre 70% completely unobstructed at 16:9, 4:3, and ultrawide. | 2026-11-21 |
| **DEC-04** | §3.2 (Typeface stand-ins) | `[D]` | Bundle open-licence **Fredoka** (700 Bold, Latin `.woff2`, SIL OFL 1.1) for chunky rounded geometric display numerals/titles and **Barlow** (500 Medium / 700 Bold, Latin `.woff2`, SIL OFL 1.1) as the DIN-style body/menu sans. Zero external CDN fetches. | `--ui-font-display`, `--ui-font-body` | Closely matches the proportions of chunky rounded Mario display lettering + DIN 2014 interface type while staying 100% clean-room and self-contained offline. | 2026-11-21 |
| **DEC-05** | §3.4 & §8 (Timing & easing curves) | `[D]` | Counter roll `320ms` (`250–400ms`), pop scale `1.20` settling in `150ms`, idle fade `260ms` (`200–300ms`) after `3500ms` idle (opt-in toggle, off by default in platformer courses per §3.1.5), pause toggle `80ms` (`< 100ms`), screen transition wipe/fade `380ms` (`<= 500ms` input-blocking budget). Ease-out: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`; ease-in: `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)`. | `--ui-motion-tick`, `--ui-motion-pop`, `--ui-motion-fade-idle`, `--ui-motion-pause`, `--ui-motion-transition`, `--ui-ease-out`, `--ui-ease-in` | Midpoints of every spec range; guarantees snappy Nintendo-grade responsiveness and zero input delay past 500ms. | 2026-11-21 |
| **DEC-06** | §3.1.1 & §3.2 (HUD numeral size & outline) | `[D]` | HUD numerals sized at `clamp(1.35rem, 5vh, 2.5rem)` (`>= 5%` viewport height) with `2.5px` dark outline (`paint-order: stroke fill` / multi-offset text-shadow) + `3px` consistent bottom-right drop shadow, multiplied cleanly by `--ui-text-scale` (`1.0` / `1.15` / `1.3`). | `--ui-size-hud-number`, `--ui-outline-width`, `--ui-shadow-offset`, `--ui-text-scale` | Ensures >= 3:1 numeral contrast over bright sky or dark cavern frames at 720p, 1080p, and maximum text scale. | 2026-11-21 |
| **DEC-07** | §1 P4 & Phase 5 (Concept-specific flourish) | `[D]` | Concept identity: **Prism Stride — Field Atlas**. A faceted **Prism Crest** SVG emblem + radiant diamond banner motif appears ONLY on Title/File Select, World Map, and Results screens. In-play HUD has zero ornament. | `TOKENS.motif.*` | Gives the game a cohesive, original identity while obeying §1 P4 and Phase 5 restraint rules. | 2026-11-21 |
| **DEC-08** | §3.7 (Failure & soft penalty) | `[D]` | On losing a life, player respawns at the last checkpoint within `1.2s` (`<= 2s`). If lives reach `0`, a soft Game Over card offers immediate 1-press Retry with 5 restored lives and a soft penalty of `min(50, floor(prismPetals * 0.25))` Prism Petals (capped at 50, never soft-locking). | `TOKENS.rules.respawnDelayMs = 1200`, `TOKENS.rules.gameOverRetryLives = 5`, `TOKENS.rules.gameOverPetalPenaltyCap = 50` | Matches SMBW/SM3DW failure philosophy (§1 P2, §3.7) so failure stings gently without ever halting the session. | 2026-11-21 |
| **DEC-09** | §3.8 (Multiplayer layout) | `[D]` | Local 1–4P support replicates a compact per-player Survival cluster (portrait badge + wedge LifeMeter + player colour ring) docked along the top/bottom safe margins while keeping a single shared Lives counter (`SHARED xN`), shared Wealth counters, and clear 70% centre playfield at 16:9 and 4:3. | `TOKENS.players[0..3]`, `.ui-v2-mp-strip` | Obeys §3.1.4 ("replicate a compact per-player cluster, not shrink the single-player HUD") and §3.8 ("shared lives, per-player colour and icon"). | 2026-11-21 |
| **DEC-10** | §3.6 (Pause Menu instant response) | `[D]` | Pause opens in `< 16ms` (immediate React render + `80ms` opacity/scale entrance) via `Escape` or `P` or gamepad `Options`, dims the canvas with solid `rgba` scrim (zero `backdrop-filter` for WebGL performance), preserves exact simulation state, displays the course's 3 Star Medallion slots (including ghosted-from-checkpoint state), Gold Shards, Prism Petals, and equipped Stride Charm. | `--ui-motion-pause`, `--ui-z-pause`, `--ui-color-scrim` | Satisfies §7 acceptance ("Pause works in under 100 ms and shows collectible status") and WebGL zero-backdrop-filter discipline. | 2026-11-21 |
